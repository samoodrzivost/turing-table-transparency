# The Turing Table — Episode 2

Theme: Things you're not supposed to say out loud

Format: The Floor. Vera asks; each chair opens (two openings, not on the clock); cross-examination, 2 exchanges each way; the open floor, where a turn ends in `>> YIELD`, `>> HOLD`, `>> ASK <name>` or `>> CONCEDE` and the other chair may cut in on a hold; one concession line each; Vera closes the question. Each chair has 90 s for cross-examination and the floor together. Seconds are estimated from the words spoken at the chair's measured rate and deducted after the turn; under 3 s a chair may not start another turn and the studio says "That's time" — a line with no model behind it. Vera referees after every panelist turn: `>> PASS` (nothing said) or one line and `>> MOVE <key>`, always with `>> HEAT 1–5`.

Every word below is the model's raw output for the aired take, unedited, minus the trailing tag lines (`>> …`), which are not spoken; they are shown on each line's label here and kept verbatim in `record.json` beside this file, with the prompts, parameters and full per-turn record.
